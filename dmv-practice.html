What to study first

Start with the signs you confuse, then check your state's rules. These are different tasks: a national practice round cannot replace your handbook.

How to use these pages

Start with the general road-sign quiz, drill regulatory signs if you miss rule signs, then choose a state page for official handbook context and a mixed permit-practice round.

The no-random-practice loop

After a missed question, check its explanation and the matching handbook section. If you confused two signs, use the contrast lab before trying a mixed round. Keep a short record of the distinction you missed, and use the checklist to prepare documents and appointment details separately.

Choose the right practice

The national round checks recognition; the contrast lab explains common confusions. State pages add local context. None is a substitute for the full handbook.

What to do after a missed sign

Do not simply retake the same quiz. Decide whether the miss was a sign-family problem, a right-of-way rule, a direction-control sign, a speed sign, or a document and appointment misunderstanding, then open the matching drill or checklist.
