Prepared: July 14, 2026

## Objective

Earn the first relevant, non-owned links to TestDayTools. Search Console currently reports zero external links, so distribution is a higher-priority ranking lever than adding another batch of state pages.

## Resource 1: DMV Road Signs Cheat Sheet

- URL: https://testdaytools.com/dmv-road-signs-cheat-sheet.html
- Title: Free DMV Road Signs Cheat Sheet: Printable Signs and Meanings
- Short description: A free, no-signup study sheet with 23 original U.S. road-sign illustrations, plain-English meanings, shape and color cues, and links to picture practice and flashcards.
- Primary audience: High school students, new drivers, driver-education teachers, and families preparing for a learner-permit test.
- Suggested material type: Reference material or drill and practice
- Language: English
- Cost: Free; no email or account required
- Accessibility note: Sign names and meanings are provided as text, controls are keyboard accessible, and the print layout duplicates information available on the web page.

## Priority Submission Channels

### 1. MERLOT

MERLOT allows members to add a publicly available online learning material by URL. Use **Add a Material**, then enter the direct resource URL and the metadata above.

- Best first submission: DMV Road Signs Cheat Sheet
- Evidence page: https://info.merlot.org/merlothelp/Add_a_Material.htm
- Account needed: Yes, free
- Expected value: Relevant education-domain listing and potential discovery by teachers and learners

### 2. OER Commons

OER Commons accepts externally hosted educational resources through **Create > Submit from Web**, followed by review and approval.

- Best first submission: DMV Road Signs Cheat Sheet
- Second submission after the first is accepted: SAT Dates Calendar
- Evidence page: https://help.oercommons.org/support/solutions/articles/42000046849-submit-oer
- Account needed: Yes
- Open-license decision needed: Yes. Do not select a Creative Commons license until the site owner decides whether others may legally reuse and adapt the illustrations and text.

### 3. Direct educator outreach

Send only personalized messages to driver-education programs, school counselors, and college-access nonprofits that already maintain free resource pages. Do not mass-submit generic directories.

Subject: Free no-signup road-sign sheet for your learner-permit resources

Message:

> I maintain TestDayTools, an independent study site for DMV and college-test planning. We created a free printable road-sign sheet with 23 original illustrations and plain-English meanings. It requires no account or email and links directly into a picture practice test. If it fits your learner-permit resource page, you are welcome to share it: https://testdaytools.com/dmv-road-signs-cheat-sheet.html. I would also be glad to correct or adapt any wording that is unclear for your students.

## Success Gate

Within 14 days of publication:

- Submit one resource to MERLOT.
- Decide whether to apply an open content license, then submit to OER Commons if approved.
- Contact five relevant organizations with individualized messages.
- Count success as at least two links from non-owned domains or one accepted education-directory listing plus one qualified reply.

Do not judge the strategy by Search Console link counts inside the first 48 hours; external-link discovery can lag the actual listing.
