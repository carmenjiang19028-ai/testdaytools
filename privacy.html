Privacy Policy

Privacy policy for TestDayTools. The site does not collect personal information or store quiz answers.

Last updated: July 14, 2026

Personal information

This site does not ask for names, emails, account numbers, scores, driver's license numbers, or other personal information.

Quiz answers

Practice quiz answers are checked in your browser. They are not submitted to a server by this static site.

Checklist and mistake-log storage

Some tools can remember selected states, checked items, or saved weak areas in your browser's local storage. This is meant only for convenience on the same device and is not an official DMV record.

No driver's license data

Do not enter driver's license numbers, Social Security numbers, birth dates, addresses, payment details, or appointment confirmation numbers into this site. Use official agency pages for personal applications.

External official-source links

Many pages link to official DMV, College Board, or other agency resources. Those sites have their own privacy practices and may ask for account or application information.

Server logs

Because the site is hosted as static pages, the hosting provider may process basic technical logs such as page requests, device information, or IP-derived region for security and delivery.

Google Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to measure basic site usage, including page views, traffic sources, device and browser information, approximate region, scrolling, and outbound clicks. This helps decide which study tools and pages need improvement. TestDayTools does not ask for names, emails, driver's license numbers, account details, or test scores.

Advertising

Advertising is not installed yet. If Google AdSense or another advertising partner is activated, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, limit, personalize, and measure ads under their own policies. TestDayTools will review this notice before ad code goes live and will use an appropriate consent message where required.

Advertising choices and consent

If advertising is activated, visitors in regions that require consent will be shown a consent or privacy-choice message before eligible advertising cookies are used. Visitors can also manage Google ad personalization through Google's advertising settings.

Children and students

The site is informational and does not ask students to create accounts, submit scores, or provide school records.

TestDayTools is fan-made and unofficial. We are not affiliated with College Board, any state DMV, or any government agency.
