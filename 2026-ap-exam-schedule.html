FAQ

Is this the official AP exam calendar?

No. This is an unofficial planning page that summarizes key windows. Use College Board and your school for official details.

Can my school change my start time?

Schools manage local logistics, rooms, and check-in procedures. Confirm exact arrival instructions with your AP coordinator.

What if I miss an AP exam?

Ask your AP coordinator immediately. Late testing depends on approved reasons and school arrangements.
