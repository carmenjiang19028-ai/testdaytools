How to use this schedule

Use this page as a planning overview, then confirm the exact subject-by-subject schedule with your school or the official AP exam calendar.

Avoid schedule mistakes

Students taking multiple AP exams should check for same-day exams, transportation timing, lunch plans, calculator rules, and any school-specific check-in instructions.

If two exams feel too close together

Back-to-back exam days can be tiring even when they do not conflict. Write down travel, meals, sleep, calculator charging, and review time for each exam instead of treating the week as one long study block.

Late testing is not a backup plan for convenience

Late testing is handled through schools and approved situations. If you think you need late testing, contact your AP coordinator early and use official school instructions.

FAQ

Is this the official AP exam calendar?

No. This is an unofficial planning page that summarizes key windows. Use College Board and your school for official details.

Can my school change my start time?

Schools manage local logistics, rooms, and check-in procedures. Confirm exact arrival instructions with your AP coordinator.

What if I miss an AP exam?

Ask your AP coordinator immediately. Late testing depends on approved reasons and school arrangements.
