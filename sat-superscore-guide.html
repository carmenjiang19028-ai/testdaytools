One student's two score reports

In the worked example, the 1,360 second sitting is the best single SAT. The 680 Reading and Writing score from sitting 1 and the 710 Math score from sitting 2 add to 1,390. That is a 30-point difference for a college that combines these dates. No score report in the example contains a 1,390 total.

Score Choice sends complete sittings

College Board sends SAT scores by test date, not by section. You cannot send only the 680 from sitting 1 and only the 710 from sitting 2. If a college needs official reports to use both highs in this example, the two complete sittings contain that evidence. Follow the institution's instructions if it asks for all dates or accepts self-reported scores first.

Check evaluation and reporting separately

First check how the college evaluates the SAT: highest sections across dates, one sitting, or another rule. Then check how it wants the scores reported: self-report or official reports, selected dates or all dates. A school can combine the highest sections and still require complete reports.

Two policy examples, not a college list

Yale's undergraduate testing page permits SAT superscores; Georgetown's first-year preparation page says it does not accept superscores. The same score reports can therefore produce different comparisons. Use the links in Sources to confirm the rule for your program and application round before sending scores.

Make a retake earn its place

Write down the section gain that would help under the verified policy. For a college considering one sitting, a stronger section alone does not guarantee a better total. Make sure the college accepts the next test date and that scores can arrive by its receipt deadline. A target is not a prediction.

FAQ

What is SAT superscoring?

Superscoring usually means a college combines your best section scores from different test dates, but each college sets its own policy.

Does every college superscore the SAT?

No. Policies vary, so verify each college's admissions or testing policy.

Should I send all SAT scores?

Not automatically. Check each college's rules for score choice, all scores, self-reporting, and official reports.

Can I send just one SAT section from each date?

No. College Board sends complete test sittings. A college that superscores can use the highest sections from the reports it receives or allows you to self-report.

Can superscoring make a lower total score useful?

Yes. If one section improves, that test date may still help at colleges that combine the best section scores across dates.
