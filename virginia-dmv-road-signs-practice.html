FAQ

How many road-sign questions must I get right in Virginia?

Virginia DMV requires all 10 questions in Part 1 to be correct. Part 2 is 30 general questions, with 24 correct required.

What is the Virginia retake rule for applicants under 18?

The DMV says applicants under 18 must wait a full 15 days before retaking; adults may test once per day.

Where does Virginia publish sample questions?

The DMV’s interactive Driver’s Manual contains practice questions and a dedicated signs and pavement-markings section.
