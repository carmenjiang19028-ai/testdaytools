How to use this before a DMV permit test

Start with the state selector, open the official source, then use the practice and checklist links as supporting tools. If the official source and this page ever differ, the official source wins.

Which section should you study next?

  • Georgia: A practice result of 16/20 in rules and 14/20 in signs is short on signs. Review signs rather than relying on the combined 30/40.
  • Virginia: A practice result of 9/10 in signs is short even if you got all the general questions right in a separate drill. Review the missed sign before the general section.
  • Arizona and Ohio: A 75% score meets the Ohio knowledge cutoff in a 40-question round but falls short of Arizona's 80% cutoff. Use the rule for the test you are actually taking.

FAQ

Are these DMV permit-test requirements official?

No. This is an independent planning tool. Use the linked DMV, DPS, MVC, PennDOT, FLHSMV, or Secretary of State source for final requirements.

Why do some states show a practice target instead of an official score?

The site keeps the practice target separate from official rules. A practice target helps you decide whether to study more, while the agency source controls the real test requirement.

What should I do after checking my state requirements?

Open the official source for the full exam scope. Use the mixed practice if it is offered for your state, or use the sign drill just for sign recognition. Then confirm documents, appointment, fees, and retake rules.
