Three Bluebook device problems to solve before the SAT

These examples are fictional. Check the requirements for your actual device and follow your school's instructions for SAT School Day.

Your Windows 10 laptop is not ready, and the SAT is in 18 days

You can study on your Windows 10 laptop, but it does not meet the current Windows requirement for Bluebook. Can you count on a College Board loaner for this weekend date?

Show reasoning
  1. Do not plan to test on Windows 10. Check an alternative device now: for example, ask your school or someone you know about a supported device you can prepare before test day.
  2. The standard College Board loaner deadline for this date has already passed. If you cannot arrange an approved device, check College Board's help or a later date before assuming you can test.

Opening a prep website does not show that the device can run the test. The lending deadline is separate from the registration and exam-setup windows.

What changes if the test is 36 days away and you still have no approved device?

Check

You have time to follow the SAT Weekend lending steps: register, complete the request in My SAT at least 30 days before testing, and arrange the required non-family adult verification. A request still does not guarantee approval.

Your school-managed Chromebook fails Verified Mode

Your school Chromebook opens Bluebook, but Test Your Device shows Verified Mode is not enabled. What should you do?

Show reasoning
  1. Tell your school's technology team what Bluebook reported. The school manages installation and Verified Mode; a student cannot prepare the Chromebook by installing the app independently.
  2. After the school fixes it, run Test Your Device again. If it cannot be fixed in time, ask the school about an approved replacement and how to prepare it.

Being school-managed and opening the app are not enough. Do not assume a personal Chromebook is an acceptable backup.

What if this is SAT School Day rather than SAT Weekend?

Check

Follow your school's device and sign-in instructions. The College Board request process for a weekend loaner does not replace the school's plan.

You need a replacement after generating your admission ticket

You completed SAT Weekend exam setup on a borrowed Mac. Two days before testing, it breaks. A supported iPad is available. Is your earlier ticket enough to prepare the iPad?

Show reasoning
  1. Check the replacement against current Bluebook requirements. Open the app and run Test Your Device on the iPad.
  2. Sign in and complete exam setup on that replacement. Check that you have your current admission ticket for test day. Do not assume the old device's setup carried over.

A ticket is not proof that the replacement device is ready. Setup must be completed on the device you use.

What if the device fails after timed testing has started?

Check

Tell your proctor immediately. Let staff direct any device swap; do not start another test or independently switch devices.

Start with approved-device rules

Check the device type, operating system, and launch settings before troubleshooting. A practice result is not an approval for test day. The table is a November 20, 2026 snapshot of minimum software versions; verify current requirements, storage and battery guidance with Bluebook.

Open Bluebook before the final night

For SAT Weekend, College Board recommends downloading and opening Bluebook at least 30 days ahead. Run Test Your Device on the machine you expect to bring. If a supported personal device still fails, note the error and ask Bluebook support. For SAT School Day, follow your school's directions for the device, app and sign-in.

Avoid last-minute system changes

If the device is already compatible and Bluebook passes its check, avoid unnecessary operating system changes close to testing. If a required upgrade or app fix is needed, confirm it with Bluebook or school technology staff and run the check again after the change.

Make a backup conversation early

For SAT Weekend, first ask your school, family, or a friend about an approved device. If you need College Board device lending, register and submit the request in My SAT at least 30 days before the test. Arrange verification by an adult who is not a family member, such as a teacher or counselor. A request does not guarantee approval. If approved, arrive 30 minutes before standard check-in to collect the loaner, complete Bluebook exam setup, and get your admission ticket.

Changing devices before test day

Complete SAT Weekend exam setup 1-5 days before testing on the approved device you will actually use, unless you have an approved College Board pickup loaner. If you change devices before arrival, open Bluebook and complete setup on the replacement; bring your current admission ticket. A ticket from earlier setup does not prepare the new device.

If Bluebook fails after the test starts

If time is still running, get the proctor's attention. Do not switch devices or start another test yourself. The proctor can decide whether a supervised swap is appropriate. If time has ended, keep the original device and follow the submission steps below.

If Bluebook has not sent your SAT Weekend answers

Bluebook normally sends your answers when time ends. Keep your device open and wait for the proctor to dismiss you. If you're testing on SAT School Day, follow your school's instructions instead.

In the room

Answer Submission Pending

Do not retry from any device. Show the screen to the proctor and follow their dismissal instructions.

Answer Submission Incomplete or an internet error

Raise your hand for help. Do not switch devices after time ends: your answers are saved on the device you finished on. Using a College Board loaner? Tell staff and stay at the test center until your answers are submitted.

After dismissal

You still need to submit answers

Reconnect on the device you finished on and sign in to Bluebook. Choose Submit Answers under Your Tests, or check Past for a submission confirmation. Act now: the SAT Weekend rules give you until 11:59 p.m. local time the next day if you were offline at the end of the test.

FAQ

Can I use my phone for the Digital SAT?

No. Official Bluebook approved-device guidance says mobile phones are not supported.

Can I use a personal Chromebook?

Official guidance says personal Chromebooks are not supported; school-managed Chromebooks may be supported.

What if my battery is weak?

Bring power according to official rules and talk to the test center if you have concerns.

When should I complete device setup?

For SAT Weekend, complete exam setup 1-5 days before testing on your approved device. If College Board has approved a test-center loaner, complete setup at pickup instead. Follow school instructions for SAT School Day.

Should I install Bluebook right before the SAT?

No. Install and open it early so you have time to fix device, login, or update problems.

What if my device is not approved for the Digital SAT?

Use official Bluebook guidance and arrange an approved device or support option before test day.

Can device stress affect SAT performance?

Yes. Worrying about login, battery, or setup can use attention that should be going to reading, math, and pacing.
