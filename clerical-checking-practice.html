How to decide

  • Match: every character, space, and digit is identical.
  • Not a match: at least one visible character differs.
  • Unsure: a deliberate omission flag; it is not counted as a correct decision.

Error definitions: false match = a mismatch marked as match; false mismatch = an exact match marked as mismatch; omission = Unsure.
