Corrections

Email carmen@cmpanda.com about an outdated rule, broken source link, unclear explanation, or accessibility problem. You can also use the public correction link below.

Best correction reports

The most useful reports include the page URL, the exact heading or question that seems wrong, the official source link, and a short note about why the page should change.

DMV account issues

TestDayTools cannot access anyone's licensing application, appointment, payment, or test result. For those records contact the licensing agency directly.

Correction reports are public

GitHub issue reports are public. Do not include names, email addresses, account details, test scores, driver's license information, school records, appointment details, or other personal information.

Advertising and privacy

Advertising is not installed yet. If ads are approved, placements will stay away from quiz answer buttons and required tool controls, and the Privacy Policy and consent settings will be reviewed before launch.
