Corrections

If you find an outdated date, broken source link, unclear explanation, or DMV-source mismatch, use the public project page until a dedicated domain email is connected.

Best correction reports

The most useful reports include the page URL, the exact heading or question that seems wrong, the official source link, and a short note about why the page should change.

DMV and exam account issues

TestDayTools cannot access DMV, DPS, MVC, FLHSMV, College Board, SAT, AP, testing-provider, appointment, payment, or score accounts. Use official support channels for personal records.

What to include

For a correction request, include the page URL, the line or section that seems wrong, and the official source you believe should be used.

No official support

For official AP, SAT, score, registration, permit, or license questions, contact College Board or the relevant DMV directly.

Advertising and privacy

Real advertising is not installed yet. If ads are approved later, placements should stay away from quiz answer buttons and the Privacy Policy should be reviewed before launch.
