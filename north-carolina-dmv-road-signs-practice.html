FAQ

How does North Carolina assess road signs?

NCDMV says applicants identify regulatory and warning signs by color and shape and provide an explanation of each.

When can a regular Class C applicant retake a failed test?

NCDMV says an applicant who fails the regular Class C knowledge or driving test may retake it after seven calendar days.

Where are North Carolina’s official sign study materials?

NCDMV’s Handbooks page links the Driver Handbook and separate Regulatory Signs and Warning Signs pages.
