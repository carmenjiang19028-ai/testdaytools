Bluebook setup timeline

  1. 30+ days beforeConfirm approved device

    Check your operating system and run Test Your Device in Bluebook. An SAT Weekend lending request must be submitted at least 30 days before testing if you need a College Board device.

  2. After the device checkResolve installation or access

    On a school-managed Chromebook, contact school technology staff about app deployment or Verified Mode. Do not wait for the test center to fix management settings.

  3. 1-5 daysComplete exam setup

    For SAT Weekend, complete exam setup on the device you will bring and get the current admission ticket. If College Board approved a loaner at the test center, complete those steps at pickup.

  4. Test dayBring power

    Arrive with the device charged and pack the power cord or permitted charger.

Bluebook is not just a download

The app, your account, your device password, battery, Wi-Fi expectations, and admission ticket flow all matter. Treat setup as a test material.

Approved devices matter

Approved device types include eligible Windows laptops and tablets, Mac laptops, iPads, and school-managed Chromebooks. Software versions and settings also matter. For SAT School Day, follow your school's instructions rather than assuming the SAT Weekend setup or lending process applies.

Battery planning is part of readiness

Official device guidance says devices must hold a charge for 3-4 hours and recommends bringing a power cord or portable charger, though outlet access may not be guaranteed.

Bluebook mistakes to avoid

Device

Borrowing a device too late

If you need a device, check official borrowing timelines early instead of waiting until test week.

Ticket

Skipping exam setup

Do not wait until test morning to set up a device you will bring. With an approved College Board loaner, complete that step at pickup.

Updates

Changing the system at the last minute

A new operating system version can create avoidable uncertainty before the SAT.

FAQ

Can I use a phone for the digital SAT?

No. College Board's Bluebook approved-device guidance says Bluebook cannot run on a mobile phone.

Can I use a personal Chromebook?

No. College Board says Bluebook can run on a school-managed Chromebook, but not a personal Chromebook.

How long should my battery last?

College Board's approved-device guidance says a device must hold a charge for 3-4 hours and recommends bringing power.

When do I print my SAT admission ticket?

When bringing a device for SAT Weekend, complete Bluebook setup 1-5 days before testing, then print the generated ticket. An approved College Board loaner is set up at pickup, where the ticket is generated instead. Follow your coordinator for approved paper testing.
