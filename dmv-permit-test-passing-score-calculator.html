Three practice scores that need different next steps

New York: 14 of 20, but only 1 of 4 signs

The overall target is met, but the sign requirement is not. The one sign answer is already included in the 14; do not add it again. Review signs before repeating the whole test.

Georgia: 14 of 20 on rules and 18 of 20 on signs

The 32 correct answers combined do not replace Georgia's two 15-of-20 requirements. Keep the sign score and practice the road rules you missed.

Virginia: 9 of 10 signs, no general score

Virginia requires all 10 signs before the general section. Leave the general score blank if you only took signs. A section not taken is not a score of zero.

Work out the miss limit by hand

If the state gives a percentage, multiply your actual test length by that percentage, round the required correct answers up to a whole number, then subtract that number from the test length.

Example: 37 questions at an 80% threshold. The threshold is 29.6, so you need at least 30 correct and can have up to 7 misses. This is a math example, not an official test length.

Use the published minimum correct answers for a fixed-length test. Check any separate sign condition before relying on the overall number.

Why the miss count is not enough by itself

A state may have a separate sign rule, a minimum question count, or a different process by applicant type. Treat the miss count as planning math, then open the state source before making test-day decisions.

Example: New York, 16 out of 20 overall and 1 out of 4 on signs. The overall score clears 14, but the result still misses the sign requirement by one. Review the missed signs, then try another round that scores signs separately. The sign answers are already part of the 16, so do not add them again. New York DMV written-test rule.

FAQ

How many questions can I miss on the DMV permit test?

It depends on the state. For example, Florida and New Jersey use 40 correct out of 50, so the basic miss count is 10. Pennsylvania uses 15 correct out of 18, so the basic miss count is 3.

Why does New York mention road-sign questions separately?

New York's learner permit rule includes both an overall score and a road-sign condition. You need at least 14 correct overall and at least 2 of the 4 road-sign questions.

Do Georgia and Virginia use one combined passing score?

No. Georgia requires 15 correct on each of its two 20-question parts. Virginia requires all 10 sign questions correct before 24 correct answers on the 30-question general section.

Can a practice score guarantee I will pass?

No. A practice score is a readiness signal, not an official result. Use the state source for final rules and retake one weak-area round before test day.
