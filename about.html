Last updated: November 24, 2026

Who operates TestDayTools

TestDayTools is operated by jiangxianxian. The site provides DMV permit test practice, state handbook guides, road-sign study materials, and application checklists. Contact: carmen@cmpanda.com.

AI-assisted production

AI-assisted tools help draft material and build illustrations and tools. Facts and practice explanations are checked manually against official state driver handbooks and agency resources. Questions are original study scenarios, not official test items.

Sources for state guides

The guides use official handbooks from California DMV, Florida FLHSMV, Illinois Secretary of State, Michigan Secretary of State, New Jersey MVC, New York DMV, PennDOT, and Texas DPS. Each guide names the handbook and links to its sources.

Corrections and updates

The page shows when its content was last updated. We revisit the affected material when a cited agency changes its guidance or a reader flags a possible error. Email the page URL, the statement or question at issue, and the official source if available.
