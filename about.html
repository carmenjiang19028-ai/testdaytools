About TestDayTools

Learn what TestDayTools is, how the site is maintained, and why all tools are unofficial.

Last updated: May 13, 2026

What we make

TestDayTools is a small, independent website that turns test logistics into quick tools: dates, checklists, score guides, and practice pages.

How we work

Pages are built from simple data files so dates, FAQs, checklists, and practice questions can be updated without redesigning the site.

Editorial approach

The site favors practical planning details: what to check, what to bring, what to verify, and which official source should make the final call.

Corrections and updates

Dates and policies can change. When a page depends on official timing, the source link is included so readers can verify the latest rule before acting.

Affiliation

We are not affiliated with College Board, AP, SAT, any DMV, or any government agency. Names are used only to describe the exams and public services students are researching.

TestDayTools is fan-made and unofficial. We are not affiliated with College Board, any state DMV, or any government agency.
