Choose a date with a backup plan

A good SAT plan usually includes one primary date and one possible retake date. That gives you room for illness, technical issues, a lower-than-expected score, or a schedule conflict.

2027 SAT dates at a glance

The confirmed spring 2027 weekend SAT dates are March 6, May 1, and June 5. March gives juniors the most retake room, May can work after more preparation, and June can help students who want a score before senior-year planning.

Do not ignore school workload

The best date on paper may be a poor date if it lands beside AP exams, finals, major travel, or a heavy activity season. A slightly later test with better preparation can be smarter than a rushed early test.

SAT School Day 2027 is different from weekend registration

The official spring 2027 SAT School Day testing window is March 1-April 30, 2027. Participating U.S. schools and districts choose whether to offer it and select a school day within that window, so ask your counselor or principal for your exact date. The fall 2026 School Day window is October 1-30, 2026.

FAQ

Are these dates guaranteed?

They are based on College Board's confirmed SAT weekend schedule as of the last page update, but registration details, test centers, and deadlines should still be confirmed before you register.

Are these SAT dates the same for every country?

College Board lists the same weekend test dates and registration deadlines for U.S. and international students. Test centers, available seats, fees, and local arrangements can differ; confirm those details in the official registration flow.

How many SAT dates should I plan for?

Many students plan one main test date and one backup retake date. Your timeline depends on deadlines and prep progress.

Should juniors test in March or May?

March gives more retake room. May may fit students who need more prep time, but it can overlap with AP exams.

Is August too early for seniors?

Not necessarily. August can be useful for seniors who prepared over the summer and want scores before fall deadlines.

Should I register before I feel fully ready?

A deadline can help your practice plan, but do not choose a date so soon that you cannot complete meaningful review.

How do I know whether a date works for my application?

Check each college's final accepted test date and score-send deadline first. Then compare the published score-release date and the study time you would have. An unexpired registration deadline does not mean the date will work for an application.

Is SAT School Day 2027 the same as the weekend SAT dates?

No. Weekend SAT dates are selected by students during registration. The spring 2027 SAT School Day window is March 1-April 30, but participating U.S. schools and districts choose whether to offer it and select the exact school day.

What are the SAT registration deadlines for 2026-2027?

Regular registration closes about two weeks before each test. The exact confirmed deadlines are August 7, August 28, September 18, October 23, and November 20, 2026, then February 19, April 16, and May 21, 2027.
