FAQ

Are these DMV permit-test requirements official?

No. This is an independent planning tool. Use the linked DMV, DPS, MVC, PennDOT, FLHSMV, or Secretary of State source for final requirements.

Why do some states show a practice target instead of an official score?

The site keeps the practice target separate from official rules. A practice target helps you decide whether to study more, while the agency source controls the real test requirement.

What should I do after checking my state requirements?

Open the official source, run one state practice round, drill road signs, then use the checklist to confirm documents, appointment, fees, and retake rules.
