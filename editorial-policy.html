Editorial Policy

How TestDayTools writes, checks, updates, and labels unofficial DMV, AP, and SAT tools.

Last updated: July 14, 2026

Purpose

TestDayTools publishes practical planning tools, checklists, and original practice questions for exam and DMV preparation. The goal is to help readers decide the next action, not to replace official sources.

Original tools and questions

Practice questions, sign illustrations, checklists, and study flows are written for this site. DMV sign graphics are simplified original SVG illustrations used for recognition practice.

User-confusion research

Forum threads, search suggestions, and trend signals can reveal what learners are actually confused about, such as course-vs-exam steps, online test results, documents, appointments, road signs, or retest rules. Those signals influence page structure, but facts still come from official sources.

No copied answer keys

The site does not copy official permit-test questions, private answer keys, Reddit comments, or competitor quiz items. Practice content is written as study material and labeled as unofficial.

Tool-first writing standard

A page should help the reader decide what to do next: open an official source, drill one weak sign group, log a mistake, check documents, or stop practicing and handle appointment logistics.

Official-source priority

When a page mentions a rule, test format, document requirement, or deadline, the official source is treated as the final authority. If a TestDayTools page conflicts with an official source, the official source wins.

Update process

Static pages are rebuilt from structured data files so dates, source links, FAQs, and practice paths can be updated without redesigning the site. Pages show a site-wide last-updated date.

Corrections

Correction requests should include the page URL, the section that appears outdated or unclear, and the official source that should be checked.

Advertising policy

Advertising is not installed yet. If ads are approved, placements will stay separate from quiz choices, checklist controls, source links, and required tool buttons. Advertising will not change practice answers, source selection, rankings, or editorial conclusions.
