    What time is the August 22, 2026 SAT?

    College Board's August 22 date page lists 7:45 a.m. local. Its standard weekend schedule says test centers generally open at 7:45 a.m., doors close at 8 a.m., and testing starts around 8:15-8:30 a.m. Your admission ticket is the final source because the center address and arrival instruction can differ.

    August registration closed on August 11

    College Board listed August 11 at 11:59 p.m. ET as the late-registration and changes deadline. If you missed it, use the September 12 or October 3 backup dates instead of planning around an unavailable August seat.

    Exam setup creates the admission ticket

    Registered students can open Bluebook 1-5 days before test day, complete exam setup, and then print or email the admission ticket. The ticket contains the center address and arrival instructions you should use.

    A physical ID and a charged device are separate gates

    An admission ticket is not a substitute for acceptable physical photo identification. Bring the approved testing device fully charged, with Bluebook installed, and pack the power cord or permitted portable charger even though outlet access is not guaranteed.
