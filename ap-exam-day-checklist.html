2027 AP subject checks

Some subjects need work before exam day or different equipment. Check any that match your courses, then confirm your setup with your AP coordinator.

Chinese, French, German, Italian, Japanese, or Spanish Language and Culture

Portfolio deadline: April 30, 2027 at 11:59 p.m. ET

Before exam day: Mark your Personalized Project Reference (PPR) as final in the AP Digital Portfolio. Verify that the school will have its printout ready for your speaking tasks.

Exam format and equipment: Fully digital in Bluebook. Confirm a wired headset with a microphone that works on your testing device. For Chinese or Japanese, also test the required input method editor before exam day.

Spanish Literature and Culture

Before exam day: Unlike Spanish Language and Culture, this exam does not require a PPR. Check who will provide your wired headset or headphones.

Exam format and equipment: Fully digital in Bluebook. You need wired audio for listening, but do not need a microphone.

Music Theory

Before exam day: Confirm wired headphones or a wired headset for listening. Separately, check how your school will provide a speaker and microphone for sight-singing.

Exam format and equipment: Hybrid in Bluebook. Write the other free responses in the paper booklet. For sight-singing, use the speaker and microphone, not the headset, and record in Bluebook.

Computer Science Principles or Seminar

Portfolio deadline: April 30, 2027 at 11:59 p.m. ET

Before exam day: Submit the required Create task components for Computer Science Principles or performance tasks for Seminar as final in the AP Digital Portfolio.

Exam format and equipment: You still have a fully digital end-of-course exam in Bluebook. The portfolio submission does not replace it.

Research

Portfolio deadline: April 30, 2027 at 11:59 p.m. ET

Before exam day: Submit your academic paper as final in the AP Digital Portfolio. Confirm your presentation and oral defense arrangements with your teacher.

Exam format and equipment: No end-of-course Bluebook exam for AP Research.

Art and Design (2-D, 3-D, or Drawing)

Portfolio deadline: May 7, 2027 at 11:59 p.m. ET

Before exam day: Submit all three required portfolio components as final in the AP Digital Portfolio.

Exam format and equipment: No end-of-course Bluebook exam for AP Art and Design.

For the audio exams above: Do not substitute Bluetooth audio. At an international test center, do not bring a personal headset for these exams. Ask what the center supplies and test any permitted adapter on the actual device.

Check Bluebook access before exam day

For digital or hybrid AP, sign in with your College Board student account and look for the exam under Your Tests. Know your sign-in details: a saved password on the device is not enough on exam day. Try the exam preview when available. Prepare for 4 hours of battery use; bring the compatible power cord, but do not assume an outlet will be available.

Fully digital and hybrid are not the same packing plan

For fully digital AP, enter all answers in Bluebook. In hybrid AP, enter multiple-choice answers in Bluebook and write free-response answers in an exam booklet where instructed. AP Music Theory also records its sight-singing response in Bluebook in 2027; check who will provide your required wired audio equipment. On a tablet, an external keyboard is required for fully digital AP and recommended for hybrid AP; external keyboards are not permitted with a laptop.

Confirm the 2027 session before planning your arrival

Look up your subject's Session 1 or Session 2 on the official exam schedule, then check your school's local start time. Some locations changed in 2027: for example, Alaska's sessions start at 7 a.m. and 11 a.m. local time. Use the reporting time and room from your AP coordinator rather than assuming the published start is the time to arrive.

Check calculator type and section, not just the subject

For 2027 AP Biology and Environmental Science, leave handheld graphing calculators at home; use a permitted nongraphing calculator or the built-in scientific calculator. For AP Statistics, a nongraphing model is not allowed, even if it has statistics functions. For Calculus AB, Calculus BC, and Precalculus, the built-in graphing tool is only available in calculator-required parts. Practice the no-calculator parts separately.

Example: AP Statistics at another school

Confirm that school's reporting time and room, put a government-issued or school-issued photo ID in your bag, and check which device the school expects you to use. For the fully digital 2027 exam, practice with Bluebook's graphing calculator if your only handheld is a scientific calculator. If you prefer a handheld, check the official approved graphing models before packing it.

Check that Bluebook has your answers

When testing time ends, keep the device open until the proctor dismisses you. A successful submission needs no retry. For hybrid AP, handing in the paper booklet does not confirm that your Bluebook answers were sent.

In the room: pending

The screen says Answer Submission Pending

Do not try to submit again, even from another device. Show the screen to the proctor and follow their dismissal instructions.

Check the official meaning of Pending
In the room: incomplete

Bluebook shows an error at the end

An incomplete submission or internet error is different from Pending. Raise your hand and follow the proctor's instructions. Do not switch devices once testing time has ended; the saved responses are on the device you finished on.

After dismissal: official email

College Board says answers are still missing

Act now: you have three calendar days after the exam. Sign in to Bluebook on the same device you finished on. Look for Submit Answers under Your Tests, or check Past for confirmation that your answers were sent. If you used a school device and no longer have it, ask your AP coordinator to set aside that device and help you submit.

FAQ

Can I bring my phone to an AP exam?

Phones are not permitted in the exam room unless preapproved as an accommodation. Ask your coordinator about storage before you arrive.

Do I need a calculator for every AP exam?

No. Check both the calculator type and the parts where you can use it. For a permitted digital part, use the built-in Bluebook calculator or an approved handheld, not a calculator on your phone or in a web browser.

Should I bring notes for last-minute review?

You can review before check-in if your school allows it, but notes should not enter the testing area unless explicitly permitted.

What should I do if I wake up sick on AP exam day?

Contact your school or AP coordinator as early as possible. Do not assume you can simply arrive late or choose a later date on your own.

Can I leave when I finish early?

Testing rooms follow official timing and school procedures. Expect to stay until the proctor releases students.
