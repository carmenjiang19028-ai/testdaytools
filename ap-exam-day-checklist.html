Pack the night before

Put approved items in one bag and remove anything you do not need. Phones, smartwatches, notes, and unapproved devices can create serious problems in a testing room.

Morning routine

Leave earlier than usual, eat something steady, and arrive with enough time for check-in. If something goes wrong, contact your school immediately instead of guessing.

Plan for the room, not the perfect room

Testing rooms can be cold, warm, quiet, crowded, or farther from your normal classroom than expected. A simple clothing layer, extra pencils, and clear arrival plan solve many avoidable problems.

Keep exam security simple

If an item is not clearly allowed, leave it outside the testing area or ask before check-in. Unapproved notes, devices, and recording tools can create bigger problems than forgetting a snack.

FAQ

Can I bring my phone to an AP exam?

Schools usually require phones to be powered off and stored away. Follow your school's instructions and official AP rules.

Do I need a calculator for every AP exam?

No. Calculator rules depend on the subject. Check the official calculator policy before exam day.

Should I bring notes for last-minute review?

You can review before check-in if your school allows it, but notes should not enter the testing area unless explicitly permitted.

What should I do if I wake up sick on AP exam day?

Contact your school or AP coordinator as early as possible. Do not assume you can simply arrive late or choose a later date on your own.

Can I leave when I finish early?

Testing rooms follow official timing and school procedures. Expect to stay until the proctor releases students.
