AP Exam Day Checklist

Use this unofficial AP exam day checklist to pack the basics, reduce morning stress, and avoid preventable testing-day issues.

Check Bluebook access before exam day

For digital or hybrid AP, sign in with your College Board student account and look for the exam under Your Tests. Know your sign-in details: a saved password on the device is not enough on exam day. Try the exam preview when available. Prepare for 4 hours of battery use; bring the compatible power cord, but do not assume an outlet will be available.

Fully digital and hybrid are not the same packing plan

For fully digital AP, enter all answers in Bluebook. In hybrid AP, enter multiple-choice answers in Bluebook and write free-response answers in an exam booklet where instructed. AP Music Theory also records its sight-singing response in Bluebook in 2027; ask your coordinator whether you need to bring a wired headset. On a tablet, an external keyboard is required for fully digital AP and recommended for hybrid AP; external keyboards are not permitted with a laptop.

Confirm the 2027 session before planning your arrival

Look up your subject's Session 1 or Session 2 on the official exam schedule, then check your school's local start time. Some locations changed in 2027: for example, Alaska's sessions start at 7 a.m. and 11 a.m. local time. Use the reporting time and room from your AP coordinator rather than assuming the published start is the time to arrive.

Check calculator type and section, not just the subject

For 2027 AP Biology and Environmental Science, leave handheld graphing calculators at home; use a permitted nongraphing calculator or the built-in scientific calculator. For AP Statistics, a nongraphing model is not allowed, even if it has statistics functions. For Calculus AB, Calculus BC, and Precalculus, the built-in graphing tool is only available in calculator-required parts. Practice the no-calculator parts separately.

Example: AP Statistics at another school

Confirm that school's reporting time and room, put a government-issued or school-issued photo ID in your bag, and check which device the school expects you to use. For the fully digital 2027 exam, practice with Bluebook's graphing calculator if your only handheld is a scientific calculator. If you prefer a handheld, check the official approved graphing models before packing it.

FAQ

Can I bring my phone to an AP exam?

Phones are not permitted in the exam room unless preapproved as an accommodation. Ask your coordinator about storage before you arrive.

Do I need a calculator for every AP exam?

No. Check both the calculator type and the parts where you can use it. For a permitted digital part, use the built-in Bluebook calculator or an approved handheld, not a calculator on your phone or in a web browser.

Should I bring notes for last-minute review?

You can review before check-in if your school allows it, but notes should not enter the testing area unless explicitly permitted.

What should I do if I wake up sick on AP exam day?

Contact your school or AP coordinator as early as possible. Do not assume you can simply arrive late or choose a later date on your own.

Can I leave when I finish early?

Testing rooms follow official timing and school procedures. Expect to stay until the proctor releases students.
