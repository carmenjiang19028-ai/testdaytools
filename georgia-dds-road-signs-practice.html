Which left-turn arrow lets you go after yielding?

A: The yellow arrow is flashing. An oncoming car is close and someone is crossing the street you want to enter. Can you turn?

Not yet. A flashing yellow arrow allows an unprotected left turn. Yield to oncoming traffic and pedestrians. Turn only when there is a safe gap and the crosswalk is clear.

B: The arrow changes to steady yellow while you are still behind the line and can stop safely. Should you start the turn?

No. Stop before the line when you can do so safely. A steady yellow arrow warns that the turn phase is ending; it does not mean the same thing as a flashing yellow arrow. If you are already in the intersection, clear it safely.

C: Your left arrow is steady red, but the lane beside you has a round green light. Can you turn left?

No. The round green is for the through lane. Stay stopped for the steady red arrow and wait for your left-turn signal to allow the turn.

Georgia knowledge exam: three decisions before you go

First visits, retests, and language requests can follow different instructions. These examples cover the non-commercial knowledge exam, not the behind-the-wheel test.

First visit

The center closes at 5 p.m. and I can arrive at 4:40

Plan an earlier visit. DDS says no appointment is needed for the initial knowledge exam, but you must be at the computer by 4:30 p.m. in this example, not just in the building. Allow time to check in and confirm the center's posted hours before leaving.

Official test-day timing
Retest

I got 14 of 20 on signs and 19 of 20 on rules

The 33 combined correct answers do not pass: the sign section needs at least 15. Use the wait to review signs, signals, and markings. For an ordinary first failure, Georgia's published rule allows a retake starting the next day; after a second or later failure, wait seven calendar days. Confirm the retest appointment, fee, and failed section with DDS, rather than relying on the initial walk-in instructions.

I want to take both parts in a language other than English

FAQ

What is the Georgia knowledge-test pass rule?

DDS says you need at least 15 correct out of 20 on both the Road Signs Test and Road Rules Test.

Does Georgia require an appointment for the knowledge exam?

DDS lists walk-in testing for the initial knowledge exam and requires candidates to be at the computer at least 30 minutes before closing. Follow DDS retest scheduling instructions if you are returning after a failure.

Where can I find official Georgia practice questions?

Georgia DDS offers a free sample test for non-commercial licenses and permits and identifies the Drivers Manual as the source material.
