Why Georgia has two knowledge-test parts

Georgia DDS describes separate Road Signs and Road Rules tests. The published minimum is 15 correct out of 20 on each part, so a strong score in one part does not replace the other.

A practical Georgia study route

Use Section 7 of the DDS Drivers Manual for signs and markings, then use DDS’s free non-commercial sample test. The actual DDS page also lists current test availability, appointment, retest, and language details.

FAQ

What is the Georgia knowledge-test pass rule?

DDS says you need at least 15 correct out of 20 on both the Road Signs Test and Road Rules Test.

Does Georgia require an appointment for the knowledge exam?

The current DDS Test and Exams Information page says no appointment is needed for the knowledge exam. Check that page for current office details.

Where can I find official Georgia practice questions?

Georgia DDS offers a free sample test for non-commercial licenses and permits and identifies the Drivers Manual as the source material.
