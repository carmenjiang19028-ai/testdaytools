Choose the steps that apply to your visit

Start with your identity documents, then follow one address route. Consent, exam proctoring, and an address certification serve different purposes; one does not automatically replace another.

Identity

Match the official document list

Use the official list for your applicant category to check identity, lawful presence, and Social Security number proof if issued. If your current legal name differs from the identity document, check which original or certified records link the names.

FLHSMV What to Bring
Address: own documents

Bring two accepted proofs in your name

Both must show where you live in Florida. Check the complete address, including the apartment number, and check the office's list before relying on a particular document or date.

Hillsborough address checklist
Address: parent or guardian

Confirm the minor's family-document route

If you are 16 and both proofs are in your parent's name, ask your issuing office what proves the relationship. For example, Hillsborough lists two proofs in the parent or guardian's name plus proof of relationship, without requiring Form 71120 for that route. Check your office before assuming it uses the same process.

Hillsborough minor address instructions
Address: another household member

Check the certification route

If you cannot provide two approved proofs in your name and live with the person whose documents you plan to use, ask about Certification of Address (HSMV 71120). For this route, the official form lists one property or lease document and one accepted supporting document in the certifier's name. The certifier signs before a notary or issuing staff. Two utility bills alone do not meet the form's two categories.

Under 18

Prepare parental consent for the application

HSMV 71142 is the application consent. Arrange for a parent or legal guardian to sign in front of licensing staff or follow the office's notarization instructions if they cannot attend. This is separate from witnessing an online exam.

Learner requirements and parental consent form
Online exam

If you tested online as a minor, check both forms

HSMV 71144 confirms that a parent or legal guardian witnessed the online exam. The form requires a notarized or examiner-witnessed signature. An electronic pass does not replace this form or the application consent. Confirm result submission with your testing provider.

Appointment

Book for the step you are actually on

For example, Orange County directs learners who have not passed to book the written test, and learners who already passed to book a driver license appointment. Choose your own office's equivalent. A pass does not issue the permit or replace vision and hearing checks; an authorized third-party pass can still be selected for a retest.

FAQ

What documents do I need for a Florida learner permit?

Start with FLHSMV What to Bring and confirm proof of identity, Social Security number, and residential address. The exact accepted documents depend on your applicant path.

Do I need an appointment after passing the Florida Class E Knowledge Exam?

Possibly. Treat the exam result and learner permit issuance as separate steps. Confirm the local service-center, county tax collector, or approved provider process before assuming you are done.

Do I need to bring a paper certificate after passing the Florida Class E exam online?

Confirm the provider has submitted the result. For a minor, the Parent Proctoring form (HSMV 71144) is separate from the test result and from the parental consent form (HSMV 71142). Follow the issuing office's instructions for each form.

Can a Florida third-party exam pass still lead to another test?

Yes. FLHSMV warns that customers who pass through an authorized third-party administrator may be selected for a mandatory random re-test without prior notice before issuance.

Is a TLSAE or DETS course completion enough to get a permit?

No. Course completion can be one requirement, but the Class E Knowledge Exam, document review, age or consent rules, fees, and permit issuance step still matter.

Should I use this checklist instead of FLHSMV?

No. Use this checklist to organize the task, then use FLHSMV What to Bring and your local office or provider instructions for final requirements.
