Ohio knowledge-test format

Ohio BMV says the knowledge test has 40 multiple-choice questions about motor-vehicle regulations and traffic signs; 75 percent correct is required to pass.

Plan a retake with the BMV rules

After a failed in-person test, Ohio requires at least a 24-hour wait. Online testing is limited to two attempts in six months. Confirm the current process on the BMV page before scheduling.

FAQ

How many questions are on Ohio’s knowledge test?

Ohio BMV lists 40 multiple-choice questions covering regulations and traffic signs, with a 75-percent passing score.

How soon can I retake the Ohio test?

The BMV says to wait at least 24 hours after a failure. Online attempts are limited to two in six months.

Which official Ohio practice materials are available?

The BMV directs applicants to the Digest of Motor Vehicles and its sample driver-license knowledge test.
