College Board AP Scores Delayed or Missing 2026

For College Board AP Exams in the U.S., if one 2026 AP score is missing, do not create a new account or panic-refresh. Some scores take longer to process, and College Board points students to AP Services if current-year scores are not received by August 15.

This page is for College Board AP scores

Use this checklist for U.S. College Board Advanced Placement exam scores. It is focused on AP score release, delayed scores, missing scores, account matching, and AP Services for Students.

Not AP EAMCET or state entrance results

If you searched for AP EAMCET 2026 result delay, Andhra Pradesh entrance results, or another non-College Board exam, this page is not the right source. Use the official state exam authority for those results.

Do the simple checks first

Most students should start by confirming they are in the same College Board account used for AP registration and class enrollment. A duplicate account can make matching slower, not faster.

Know the difference between delayed and missing

A delayed score may still be processing. A missing score after the official support checkpoint needs direct help from AP Services. This site cannot see private score records.

If a college is waiting

Check whether the college needs official AP scores right away or whether placement can be updated later. Contact the college directly for deadline-specific instructions.

FAQ

Why is one AP score missing but the others are visible?

One score can take longer because of late testing, processing, or record matching. College Board says some scores may take longer and that students will be emailed when a score is added.

Is this page about AP EAMCET result delays?

No. This page is only for U.S. College Board Advanced Placement score delays or missing AP scores. It is not about AP EAMCET or other state entrance exam results.

Should I make a new College Board account?

No. College Board warns that duplicate accounts can delay AP scores. Try to recover the account you already used.

What happens after August 15?

If you do not receive current-year AP scores by August 15, College Board directs students to contact AP Services for Students.

Will delayed scores still be sent to colleges?

College Board says delayed current-year scores may be sent automatically to designated current-year recipients when they become available, but you should verify your own situation officially.
